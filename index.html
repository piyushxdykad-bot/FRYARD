How we make it

  1. Brine overnight

    Chicken thigh and tenders sit in spiced buttermilk so the meat stays juicy right through the fry.

  2. Dredge and double fry

    Seasoned flour, a quick rest, then two rounds in the oil for the craggy crust that cracks when you bite.

  3. Dunk in cayenne oil

    Hot fryer oil whisked with cayenne, paprika and brown sugar, brushed on straight away at the heat level you chose.

  4. Bread and pickles

    Served Nashville style on white bread with crinkle-cut pickles to cool things down, and comeback sauce for dipping.
