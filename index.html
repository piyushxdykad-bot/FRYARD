From Nashville to the Night Market

drag the photos around

Nashville hot chicken is fried chicken that gets brushed with cayenne-loaded oil the second it leaves the fryer. We fell for it, then built a red stall to bring it to the Night Market.

  1. The recipe

    Overnight brine, double fry, cayenne oil, white bread and pickles. Done the Nashville way, tuned to how hot you want it.

  2. The stall

    A red stall at the Night Market, in front of Company Garden No. 1. Lights on at 6 PM, last order before 11.

  3. The regulars

    Word spread from table to table and onto Instagram, where more than 2,400 people now follow the stall.

  4. Next up

    Party trays and bulk orders, so the heat can come to you.
