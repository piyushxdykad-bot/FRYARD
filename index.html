    The legend · 1930s

    It started as payback.

    As the story goes, a man named Thornton Prince in Nashville kept coming home late. To get even, his girlfriend cooked him fried chicken loaded with every hot pepper she could find. It backfired. He loved it so much that his family opened a chicken shack, and Nashville hot chicken was born.

    Almost a century later, that same fire lands at the Night Market.
